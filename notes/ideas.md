# Ideas

Raw ideas worth exploring. Promote to [[roadmap]] when committed to.

## Local SEO (Stupava) — highest leverage

- **Google Business Profile** — the single biggest local move: Maps + local pack for "angličtina Stupava". Also the correct source of geo coordinates (deliberately left out of JSON-LD rather than fabricated) and of genuine Google reviews. Once live, add its URL + a Facebook page URL to `business.socialProfiles` (feeds schema `sameAs`).
- **Slovak directory listings** — Firmy.sk, Zoznam, Azet katalóg. Become citations that AI engines read.
- **Neighbouring-area copy** — mention Marianka, Borinka, Lozorno, Záhorská Bystrica, Malacky where students commute from.

## Content

- **Blog / articles** (3–4 Slovak posts, e.g. "Ako sa pripraviť na pracovný pohovor v angličtine", "Angličtina pre začiatočníkov v Stupave"). Ranks long-tail and gives AI engines quotable material. Trivial to add in 11ty.
